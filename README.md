# 🌬️ Breatheez – Asthma Trigger Prevention Mobile App

## 📌 Overview
**Breatheez** is a mobile application built to help asthma patients avoid poor air quality and manage their health more effectively.  
The app provides real-time air quality insights, personalized journaling, and AI-powered support to guide users in preventing asthma triggers.

---

## ✨ Features
- 🔑 **User Authentication** – Secure login and account management  
- 📓 **Health Journaling** – Track symptoms, triggers, and daily health notes  
- 🤖 **AI Chatbot (Gemini API)** – Ask health-related questions and receive instant AI-powered guidance  
- 🗺️ **Interactive Map** – Integrated with **Google Places API** and **Air Quality API** to show nearby air quality conditions and safe zones  
- 🌍 **Real-Time Air Quality Alerts** – Get notified when air quality drops below safe levels
